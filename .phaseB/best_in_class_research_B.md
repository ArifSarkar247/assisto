# Competitor research B — real user complaints & strengths (read 27 Oct 2026)

Method: Google Play listing HTML (rating/installs) + Google Play public review feed (same data shown on the listing's "See all reviews"; "Most relevant" sort and "Newest 1-star" filter, gl=BD, hl=en). Each complaint below paraphrases a real review; the URL is the app's Play listing (individual Play reviews have no permalink) and the date is the review's month. No reviewer names recorded. Web/news sources used where noted.

## TallyKhata (com.progoti.tallykhata, 4.2★, 10M+ installs, 46.6K reviews, read 27 Oct 2026)
Strengths:
- Bangla-first ledger "made in our country", free core, SMS/WhatsApp due reminders (Play reviews, Nov 2020 / Oct 2024)
- Bangla QR payment acceptance inside the ledger (TallyPay) praised (Play review, Sep 2026)
- Customer-facing tally.pe short-link so the customer can see their dues (Play review, Feb 2023)
Complaints:
1. Free features moved behind paid "Gold" subscription; constant pay pop-ups, entry limits on standard plan — https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.progoti.tallykhata — Feb 2025 (378 helpful), Oct 2025, Oct 2026
2. Ads added inside a money app; ads on every data update + promo notifications — same URL — Jul 2025, Apr 2026
3. Offline entry broken: used to save offline and back up later, now needs internet ("No internet = useless") — same URL — Jul 2025, Apr 2026
4. Multi-device / multi-person shop use not supported; second device deactivates first, sync delays 1–2 h — same URL — Feb 2021, Dec 2023, Feb 2023, Sep 2024
5. Paid-tier support slow: wrong/negative balances, "we are checking" for 12 days — same URL — Aug 2026
6. Login fails abroad (only works on BD networks) — expats in Qatar etc. can't log in — same URL — Feb 2026, Jun 2026
7. Undismissable NID-verification nag + loud voice messages; KYC demands storefront photo (online sellers rejected) — same URL — Oct 2024, Nov 2024, Jun 2026
8. Slow after updates (recharge tab 1–2 min to load) — same URL — Oct 2023
BD-specific insight: shop owners want multi-family-member logins, gallery image of bKash/Nagad payment screenshots as proof, Palli Bidyut bill pay for rural shops, fingerprint instead of PIN, English option (UI is Bangla-only). Monetisation switch (free→Gold) is the #1 trust breaker.

## bKash (com.bKash.customerapp, 4.4★, 100M+ installs, 1.7M reviews, read 27 Oct 2026)

BD-specific insight: OTP fragility + forced updates + low-end-phone slowness are the top pain; expats with foreign numbers can't log in (Sep 2026). Bill-pay "pending" with no token is a prime trust gap for a prepaid-meter assistant.

## ekPay (BD govt bill-payment aggregator, a2i/ICT Division) — no consumer Play app with reviews found (read 27 Oct 2026)
- Play package com.ekpay ("EK-Pay", 1K+ installs, 1 review) is NOT the Bangladesh ekPay; bd.com.ekapp is unrelated. BD ekPay works as a web/aggregator behind banks & MFS, so no first-hand app reviews exist.
Strengths:
- One switch for electricity, gas, water, city-corporation, education-board, BRTA fees via "any payment channel" (Financial Express, Jan 2025 — https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/trade/ekpay-aims-to-ramp-up-services ; TBS — https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/ict-division-aims-develop-inclusive-payment-ecosystem-through-ekpay-1039741 — date not shown on fetch)
- Bangladesh Bank supervision = government trust anchor (same sources)
Complaints/weaknesses (institutional, not app reviews):
1. Officials themselves acknowledge "mistrust in the ekpay platform" — https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/trade/ekpay-aims-to-ramp-up-services — Jan 2025
2. MFS interoperability gaps ("two separate entities ... cannot ensure interoperability") — same — Jan 2025
3. Low awareness; needs to be "properly promoted" — same — Jan 2025
BD-specific insight: users don't know ekPay as a brand; they pay utility bills via bKash/Nagad, where the "pending / no token" failures happen (see bKash). An assistant that tracks bill status and receipts across channels fills this gap.
