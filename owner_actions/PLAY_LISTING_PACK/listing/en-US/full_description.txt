Assisto: Chat, calls, AI assistant, safety and business, in one super app.
Many apps. One Assisto.

Assisto is an assistant that runs much of your daily life, family safety and business by itself, in Bangla and English. Type or say one line, like "call mum at 8 tomorrow" or "500 for lunch", and Assisto makes the reminder or writes the expense. Anything that sends money waits for your OK.

ASSISTANT AND PERSONAL SECRETARY
• Get things done by talking: one sentence becomes a reminder or an expense
• Assisto Voice: tap the mic to call, set an alarm, log an expense or send SOS
• Daily brief: your list for the day is ready every morning
• Nudges: Assisto spots what is urgent in your own data and tells you in time
• Personal Secretary: schedule, appointments, follow-ups and letters in one place
• See what Assisto remembers, delete any item, or keep it away from AI

MY CIRCLE: CHAT AND CALLS
• End-to-end encrypted one-to-one and group chats with the people you add
• Photos, voice notes and files, view-once photos and disappearing messages
• Encrypted voice and video calls; calls are never recorded
• Keep using your phone during a video call with picture-in-picture
• Floating chat bubbles (Android 11+), status, channels and communities

FAMILY CARE AND SAFETY
• One-tap SOS sends your location to the family members you choose; if nobody opens it, Assisto rings them again after 2 minutes
• Emergency hub: 999, the nearest police station and your emergency card
• Safe Journey: chosen people see your live location only during the trip and are alerted if you are late
• Lost or stolen phone: from another phone, lock it, sound an alarm and see the last location; alerts on a SIM change. The phone must be on and online.
• Family care, a school corner for your child and a simple home screen for elders
• Blood donors, scam check, disaster alerts and nominee access
Assisto is not an emergency service. In danger, call 999.

CCTV AND SHOP WATCH
• Shop Box: a spare Android phone becomes a shop camera. It detects on the phone and sends you one photo; no video leaves the shop.
• Smart alerts: a person in the zone you drew means a photo alert
• Live view of your own IP cameras, shared with family if you want
• Evidence pack for a police report
• End-of-day cash check against the day's sales
Some CCTV features need a paid plan.

BUSINESS
• Digital khata, free on every plan: due reminders go out on your schedule and stop once paid
• Accounts: sales, purchases and expenses in one tap; profit and loss, cash flow and VAT summary

• AI sales assistant: answers customers from your own products and prices, notes the order and hands over to you when unsure
• CRM: customers, leads, pipeline and quotes
• Run the shop: quick sell, stock with low-stock alerts, orders, cash box, staff and a daily report

WORK TOOLS
• Job hunter, CV builder, mock interview, task board, meetings and a document scanner

DAILY LIFE
• Reminders, notes, to-dos, birthdays and special days
• Money: income, expenses, budget, loans and a monthly recap; log money by voice. Assisto never holds your money.
• Subscriptions and bills, house rent, prepaid meter warnings, tax and zakat calculator
• Split expenses, mess accounts and samity records
• PIN-locked document vault, Photo Studio, exam result alerts and Ramadan mode
• Vehicle tracking with your own GPS tracker (hardware not included)
• Steps and sleep from Health Connect, only with your permission
