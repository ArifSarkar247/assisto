OWNER ORDER (design approved by owner) - MY CIRCLE GROUP CALLS. First check what already exists for group or conference calls in My Circle and reuse it. 1) ADD PERSON DURING ANY CALL: in a running 1-to-1 audio or video call add an Add person button (Bangla যোগ করুন) next to mute, speaker, video. It opens a My Circle picker with search and checkboxes; the chosen people get a normal incoming call ring (works with the app closed, full-screen ring like 1-to-1); when they answer they join the same call. 2) GROUP CALL FROM A GROUP: inside a My Circle group chat, audio and video call buttons ring all members at once; members can join late from a Join call bar in the chat while the call is live. 3) CALL SCREEN: grid of tiles up to 8 people, each with name, mic-off icon, ringing state, and a green border on the active speaker; the call goes on when someone leaves; the last two people continue as a normal call. 4) LIMIT: max 8 people in one call; show a clear Bangla message if full. 5) MONEY: audio group calls free for everyone; group video calls follow the existing rule (video is Premium). 6) Use our existing WebRTC and TURN setup; pick SFU or mesh with a short reason in STATUS, and keep data use low on mobile data. 7) Block and privacy rules apply: a blocked person can never be added; only members of My Circle can be added. 8) Design System V2, Bangla and English strings. 9) Tests: add person to audio and video, group ring with app closed, late join, leave and continue, limit 8, blocked person cannot be added, media flows both ways for every participant (same strict media proof as the 1-to-1 gate).
