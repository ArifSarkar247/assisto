# Name / Trademark research — "Assisto"

> **This is a research checklist and findings surface, NOT legal clearance.**
> No availability or clearance is claimed. Complete the steps below and consult
> a trademark attorney before finalising or filing.

## Why this matters
The app ships an in-app "About the name" note (backend: `core/nmcheck.py`,
`about_the_name()`) that shows the meaning, the relevant trademark classes, this
checklist, and a plain disclaimer. The note deliberately states
"Research pending — no clearance claimed."

## Relevant trademark (Nice) classes
| Class | Covers |
|------:|--------|
| 9  | Software, mobile apps, downloadable programs |
| 42 | SaaS, software design & development |
| 35 | Business assistance (CRM / Business-mode part) |
| 38 | Telecom / messaging services (if applicable) |

## Owner checklist (do these in order)
1. **BD DPDT** — Search "Assisto" in the Bangladesh Department of Patents,
   Designs & Trademarks database (http://www.dpdt.gov.bd/). Search the whole
   register, then within each relevant class above.
2. **Nice classes** — Repeat the DPDT search scoped to classes 9, 42, 35, 38
   individually (a mark can be free in one class and taken in another).
3. **Global** — Search the WIPO Global Brand Database
   (https://branddb.wipo.int/) and, for key export markets, USPTO (TESS) and
   EUIPO (eSearch plus).
4. **App-store collisions** — Search "Assisto" on Google Play and the Apple
   App Store. Note any existing apps with the same/similar name, especially in
   the productivity / assistant category (confusion risk + store rejection
   risk).
5. **Domain & handles** — Check availability of assisto.* domains and the
   social handles the brand will use.
6. **Attorney** — Before relying on the name or filing, have a trademark
   attorney run a clearance search and advise on registrability in Bangladesh
   (and any target export markets).
